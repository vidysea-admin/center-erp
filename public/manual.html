Center Management ERP — User Manual

The complete operational journey in one system: Location & Target → Trainer → Batch → Candidate → Enrollment → Daily Attendance → Assessment → Certificate → Invoice → Cost. Step-by-step instructions for every screen are below. Use the search box above to find anything.

Updated August 2026 — new since the first release: Sheet Watch (track the client's workbook automatically), Contacts & Meeting Notes, Backward Batch Planner & time slots, Candidate Eligibility & SIDH registration, Candidate Feedback, and self-signup with Admin approval & togglable role permissions. Newest: Government Attendance Import (upload the portal export, see where it disagrees with our logs) and the confirmed scheme timing & counting rules.

🔐 Login

The first step

  1. Open the ERP link in your browser
  2. Already have an account? Enter your email and password → Sign in — you will land on the Home screen
  3. New here? Click Create an account below the button — fill your details, choose your role (Trainer / Location SPOC / Enrollment / Operations) and submit
  4. A new account is Pending until an Admin approves it — you cannot log in before that. The Admin sees your request immediately and approves or corrects it
⚠️ If sign-in fails, either the email/password is wrong or your account is still awaiting approval. Forgot your password? Ask the Admin to reset it (Admin → Users & Access).

🏠 Home — Action Center

The first screen after login. It shows only the things that need action TODAY.

  1. The 5 cards on top — Active Locations, Active Batches, Enrolled Students, Trainer Requests, Follow-ups. Click any card to open the corresponding list
  2. Missing Daily Logs — any batch whose log for the previous working day wasn't filled appears here with the owner's name. Click a row to open that batch's Daily Execution form directly
  3. Sheet Changes Pending Review — changes detected in the AVPL sheet. Click to open the Sync Inbox
  4. Government Attendance Gap — days where our attendance and the government portal's attendance differ (−8 pts shows amber; larger gaps show red). Click to open that day's log
  5. Enrollment Failures — students whose enrollment failed (OTP issues, etc.). Click to open that batch's enrollment list
  6. Invoices Pending — invoices waiting to be raised or paid. Click to open that batch's Closure tab
💡 Start every day from Home — when these queues are empty, the day's work is done. Check the 🔔 bell too: it carries everything the system noticed on its own (see Alerts).

🔄 Sync Inbox — actionable changes from the AVPL/SDP sheet

For anyone with the "approve sheet changes" right. The external sheet is crawled on schedule; whatever changed in the fields we track lands here — nothing is ever applied automatically.

💡 Two screens, two jobs: Sheet Watch shows every cell the client touched anywhere in the workbook (a full audit of their edits). Sync Inbox is the smaller, actionable list — target and status changes that can start, hold, stop or close a location.
  1. Go to Sync Inbox in the sidebar (the blue badge shows how many changes are pending)
  2. Click any Open row — a review panel opens on the right
  3. The panel shows what changed (old → new) and the Impact — how many active batches, trainers and candidates this location has
  4. Choose an Action: No action Update target Start location Put on hold Stop location Close location
  5. Stop/Close actions require a reason
  6. Click Apply & Acknowledge
⚠️ When you Stop/Close a location, the system automatically creates follow-up tasks (stop batches, release trainers, return candidates to the pool). These appear on Home, and the change stays "Open" until every follow-up is resolved. Nothing is shut down silently.
  1. To ignore several changes at once: select their checkboxes → Bulk Ignore

📍 Locations — all centers

  1. Sidebar → Locations
  2. Each row shows: Code, Name, City, Approval status (from AVPL — Pending/Approved/Rejected), Operational status (ours — Active/On Hold/Stopped/Closed), and the SPOC
  3. To add a center: New Location → fill the form → Create. Set External ID to the center's ID in the AVPL sheet — that's how sheet rows are matched
  4. Click any row to open that location's detail page

🏫 Location Detail — 6 tabs

  1. Overview — update name, city, and the primary SPOC/Principal names and phone numbers here → Save. Changing the operational status requires a reason
  2. Contacts & Notes — see Contacts & Meeting Notes: as many contact people as the centre needs, plus a dated diary of every conversation
  3. Capacity & Target — see below 👇
  4. Trainers & Infra — add rooms (named, e.g. "Classroom 1", "Lab 1", so clashes can be detected) and raise Trainer Requests
  5. Batches — all batches at this location
  6. Activity — a full record of who changed what

🎯 Capacity & Target

  1. Each program shows its Approved target (from AVPL) and Allocated target (what we commit internally)
  2. The Capacity math appears automatically: "Target 210 → 7 batches × 20 days = 140 trainer-days → 2 trainers required within 90 days" — this tells you how many trainers are needed
  3. Achieved shows both bases side by side — how many are enrolled and how many are certified — with the remaining count, so "target mila vs kiya" is answered at a glance
  4. To set a target: choose the Program → enter the numbers → Save target

🧑‍🏫 Trainers — pool and requests

  1. Sidebar → Trainers. Two statuses are shown: Pipeline (where they are in hiring) and Status (set automatically — Assigned when a batch is allocated, otherwise Available)
  2. The hiring journey — set Pipeline as they progress: Applied → Shortlisted → TOT In Progress → Ready to Train. Once TOT is certified, record the TR ID from NSDC
  3. To add a trainer: Add Trainer → name, phone, skills (must match a program's trainer skill), pipeline stage, TR ID, and Can train at — pick every location they can travel to (one, two or ten). Leave it empty to mean "anywhere"
  4. Compensation: choose Batch-wise or Monthly, enter the fixed amount, and note the performance incentive separately
  5. Max concurrent batches is 4 by default — a trainer can run up to four batches when the day is divided into time slots
  6. Requests tab — trainer requests for locations that need one: location, program, required-by date, TOT date. Update the status right here (Open → In Progress → Fulfilled). Raising a request alerts Admin and Operations immediately
⚠️ Assigning one trainer to two batches at the same time slot is blocked outright, and so is going past their batch limit — the system names the conflicting batch and dates. Booking someone who is not yet "Ready to Train", or not listed for that location, gives you a warning (planning ahead is allowed) — but the batch still cannot start before their availability date.

🧑‍🎓 Candidates — the student pool

  1. Sidebar → Candidates. Filter by location and status (Unassigned/Assigned/Enrolled/Dropped/Completed)
  2. Three ways a candidate enters the pool:
  3. 1️⃣ Single student on a call: Add Candidate → name, phone, location, program, date of birth, education, last government training date, which programs and locations they are interested in (so you can shortlist them quickly later), source
  4. 2️⃣ Bulk import from Excel: Import Excel → first click "Download sample sheet format" to get the blank template → fill it → upload → map the columns (which column is name, which is phone) → Preview → Import. Possible duplicates are flagged, never blocked
  5. 3️⃣ Self-registration link: pick a location in the filter → Self-reg link → the link is copied to your clipboard. Share it on WhatsApp; candidates fill their own details and land straight in that location's pool
  6. Each row shows an Eligible badge and the SIDH registration status — see Eligibility & SIDH
  7. Assign to a batch: tick the students' checkboxes ✔ → Assign to Batch → pick the batch — done. If someone is not eligible you get a warning naming them, and the assignment still goes through
💡 A student can be in only one batch at a time. If you try to assign them again, the system tells you which batch they are already in.

🎓 Batches — from planning to completion

  1. Sidebar → Batches. Each row shows: code, location, status, Health (Green/Amber/Red with its reasons), Enrolled/Roster/Target, trainer, start date
  2. Plan a batch — the countdown checklist before you create anything. See Backward Batch Planner
  3. New batch: New Batch → choose Location + Program + Start date → optional time slot (e.g. 09:00–13:00, so one trainer can run parallel batches) → Trainer (only those with a matching skill appear) → Room → Create Batch
  4. The drawer shows the "Earliest possible start" (based on mobilisation lead time and trainer availability)
  5. The end date is computed automatically: start + course duration + buffer, and the backward plan checklist is attached to the batch straight away
💡 A batch's lifecycle: Planning → Ready → Active → Closing → Completed. Each next step unlocks only when the previous conditions are met.

📊 Batch Detail — Overview & readiness

  1. Opening a batch shows the Readiness checklist: ✓ Location approved, ✓ Room, ✓ Trainer, ✓ Roster ≥ 80% of target
  2. Once all four are ✓, click Mark Ready
  3. Start Batch works only when enough students are enrolled (80%, configurable) and the start date has arrived
  4. Trainer/room/dates/time slot can be edited in the Details panel — any clash is blocked by the system
  5. The Backward plan panel lists every milestone with its due date — tick them off as they finish; overdue ones turn red and raise an alert. While the batch is in Planning you can Regenerate the dates after changing the start date
  6. Cancelling requires a reason; once daily logs exist, only an Admin can force-close

Tabs: Candidates (roster + drops) · Enrollment · Daily Execution · Closure · Feedback · Costs · Activity

✅ Enrollment Worklist — the 3 portal steps

Batch Detail → Enrollment tab. Works comfortably on a phone (one card at a time).

  1. One card per student — as you complete each step on the government portal, tick it here: Registration → e-KYC → Batch Accept
  2. All three ✓ = student Completed (Enrolled) — the status updates automatically
  3. Ran into a problem? Pick the reason from the dropdown: OTP not received / Already registered / KYC failed / Portal error / Duplicate — the card is marked Failed
  4. A failure does NOT lose the student — fix the issue and click "Clear failure", or move them to another batch

📅 Daily Execution — the daily log

Batch Detail → Daily Execution. The SPOC or trainer can fill this from a phone.

  1. Pick the date (today is pre-selected)
  2. Attendance: tap the name of each student who is present — it turns green with a ✓
  3. Enter the topic covered, take photos and videos (directly from the camera; the system compresses images automatically — works on slow networks, and failed uploads go to a "Retry" queue). Upload at least twice a day — if fewer arrive, the system raises an alert the next morning
  4. Government portal present: enter the number shown on the portal + upload a screenshot as proof. Leaving it blank means "Not verified" (which is different from 0)
  5. Click Save Daily Log
  6. The History below shows each day's Internal % vs Government % and the Gap — amber (5–10 pts) / red (10+ pts). If a gap appears, get the biometric attendance verified
  7. Made a mistake? Click Edit — your own log within 48 hours; Operations/Admin any time. Every edit is recorded
⚠️ The attendance denominator is that day's roster and is frozen the moment the log is saved — if a student drops later, historical percentages do not change.

🏁 Closure — Assessment → Certificate → Invoice

Batch Detail → Closure tab

💡 Per-candidate marking (new): click Start per-candidate marking to record a result for every student instead of just totals. Tap Pass / Fail / Absent on each card (Fail asks for a reason), add scores, then use Issue certificates to enter numbers in bulk. Appeared and Passed are then calculated for you and shown as "derived" — you never type them again. A batch cannot be marked assessment-complete while anyone is still Pending, and no certificate can be issued without a Pass. Older batches keep their existing totals exactly as they were.
  1. Assessment: date, how many appeared, how many passed — Mark Completed. (Appeared cannot exceed the roster)
  2. The batch can now be moved to Move to Closing (from the Overview tab)
  3. Certification: date + number of certificates issued — Mark Completed
  4. Click Mark Ready for Invoice — the invoice automatically becomes "Ready"
  5. Enter the invoice number, date and amount, then Mark Raised; once payment arrives, Mark Paid
  6. Finally, Complete the batch — remaining students automatically become "Completed" and the trainer is released

💰 Costs & Invoices

Admin & Operations only

  1. Sidebar → Costs. Costs tab: add an entry — date, location/trainer, category (Trainer Fee, Incentive, TOT, Rent, Materials…), amount
  2. Batch-level costs are added from that batch's Costs tab, which also shows the running total
  3. Invoices tab: all invoices in one place — status, amount, dates. Raising and marking paid happens on the batch's Closure tab

⚙️ Admin — Admin role only

  1. Programs: the master list of courses — duration, buffer, batch size, completion deadline, trainer skill. All capacity math runs on these numbers
  2. Users & Access: pending signups appear in an amber box at the top — Approve, "Review & edit…" first, or Reject. You can also create users directly: pick a role, set the location scope (for Location/Trainer users), Can edit (off = view only) and any special rights. Passwords are reset here too
  3. Permissions: the roles × rights grid — tick what each role's group can do, including who may approve sheet changes. See Permissions & Signup
  4. Sync Source: two modes. Mapped = the AVPL sheet's link + column mapping (sheet column ↔ system field) that feeds the Sync Inbox — on a column mismatch the sync stops itself, so wrong data is never imported. Watch = track the client's whole workbook cell by cell (set the poll interval and which columns identify a row) which feeds Sheet Watch. OneDrive share links work as they are. "Sync Now" runs either immediately
  5. Approvals: optionally require a second person to sign off on critical actions — see Approvals
  6. Master Lists: cost categories, drop reasons, failure reasons
  7. Defaults: three groups — planning (batch size 30, duration 15+5, 90-day deadline, attendance gap thresholds, enrollment threshold, max 4 batches per trainer), candidate eligibility (min/max age, training cooldown months, minimum daily photo uploads, SIDH portal URL), and backward-plan lead times (how many days before the start each milestone is due). All configurable here, no code changes needed

🔔 Alerts

The bell in the header — conditions the system watches for you, raised automatically

  1. The bell shows a red count when something needs attention. Click it to open the Alerts page
  2. Each alert says exactly what is wrong and where — for example "B011 at Kota 02 has no daily log for 3 operating days"
  3. Colour shows urgency: red is critical, amber is a warning
  4. Open jumps straight to the screen where you can fix it
  5. Acknowledge means "I have seen this"; Resolve means "this is handled"
💡 Alerts clear themselves once the condition stops being true — enter the missing daily log and the alert disappears on its own. The system watches for: sheet changes unreviewed for 48 hours (both Sync Inbox and Sheet Watch), new signups awaiting approval, new trainer requests, trainer requests coming due, missing daily logs, too few photo/video uploads yesterday, government attendance gaps, overdue plan milestones, assessments past their date, invoices ready but unraised, and batches stuck past their start date.

✅ Approvals (optional)

Admin → Approvals — require a second person to sign off on critical actions

  1. Everything is switched OFF by default — the system behaves exactly as before until an Admin turns something on
  2. Admin → Approvals lists the six gateable actions: close/stop a location, cancel/complete a batch, mark an invoice raised/paid
  3. Tick one and choose who approves it (Admin or Operations)
  4. From then on, when someone performs that action it is not applied — it is parked and the approver is notified
  5. The approver opens Admin → Approvals, reviews the request, and clicks Approve & apply or Reject
⚠️ Nobody can approve their own request — that is the whole point of the control. Turn a switch back off at any time and that action becomes immediate again.

👁️ Sheet Watch

Sidebar → Sheet Watch — every change the client makes in the shared workbook, caught automatically

  1. The client's OneDrive workbook is downloaded automatically every 30 minutes — every tab, every column
  2. Any difference from the previous copy becomes a row here: which tab, which row (institution + job role), which column, and old value → new value
  3. Green = a row was added, red = a row was removed, amber = a cell was modified
  4. Click Seen once you have read it, Accept once it has been handled — select many rows to act in bulk
  5. The sidebar badge counts unreviewed changes; anything unreviewed for 48 hours raises an alert
💡 Accepting a change here does not change any ERP record — it only marks the change as reviewed. Location/target updates still go through the Sync Inbox or the record itself, so nothing is ever overwritten blindly.
⚠️ The sheet's TC Password column is masked for everyone except Admin.

👥 Location Contacts & Meeting Notes

Location Detail → Contacts & Notes — any number of contacts, and a diary of every conversation

  1. Add as many contact persons as a location needs — a second SPOC, the Cluster Head, any local contact — with name, phone and role
  2. The original SPOC/Principal from the master fields still show at the top, labelled "from master fields"
  3. Below that, Meeting notes: date, who you met, and what was discussed — press Add note
  4. Every note records who logged it, so Admin and Operations always see the same history of "who spoke to whom, when, about what"

📅 Backward Batch Planner & Time Slots

Batches → Plan a batch — pick a start date, get the full countdown checklist

  1. Click Plan a batch on the Batches page and choose a start date
  2. The system counts backwards: trainer identified → TOT done (at least 3 days before) → mobilization → trainer ready → registration & enrollment done (day before) — each with its due date
  3. Copy as text or Print to hand the checklist to anyone: "तेरे को batch start करना है तो ये-ये काम इस-इस date तक"
  4. Every new batch stores this checklist on its Overview tab — tick milestones off as they finish; overdue ones turn red and raise an alert
  5. All lead times are configurable in Admin → Defaults
  6. Time slots: a trainer may run up to 4 parallel batches across dates when the day is divided by time. Give each batch a slot (e.g. 09:00–13:00) — the system hard-blocks the same trainer being in two places at the same time, and enforces the scheme guidelines (9–6, max 4 hours, max two sessions a day — see Scheme Timing)

🎯 Candidate Eligibility & SIDH Registration

Candidates page — who qualifies, and getting them onto the Skill India portal

  1. Each candidate row shows an eligibility badge: Eligible (green), Not eligible (red — hover to see why), or Unverified (DOB/education not recorded yet)
  2. The rules: age 18–40, 10th pass, and no government training in the last 6 months — record the last training date and the system knows exactly when they become eligible again. Thresholds live in Admin → Defaults
  3. Assigning an ineligible candidate to a batch warns you; completing their enrollment is blocked
  4. SIDH column: WhatsApp or SMS opens that app with the Skill India registration link pre-filled — the candidate registers themselves. Click ✓ Reg. once they are registered on the portal. SMS is there because rural candidates often do not use WhatsApp
  5. Bulk SMS file downloads a phone-plus-message CSV for everyone in the current view who is not yet registered — the format a bulk-SMS gateway takes, for when sending sixty links by hand is not realistic
  6. Export for SIDH CRM downloads an Excel in the assisted-registration tool's format, for candidates who need an agent's help on the call (the Aadhaar column is left blank on purpose — the agent fills it during the call; the ERP never stores Aadhaar)
  7. Self-reg link creates a public link for a location — share it on WhatsApp and candidates fill in their own details, which land straight in your pool marked "Self Registration"

⭐ Candidate Feedback

Batch Detail → Feedback — every candidate rates their training

  1. Open a batch → Feedback tab → Get feedback links — one private link per candidate on the roster
  2. Send each link on WhatsApp with one click (the message is pre-written)
  3. The candidate opens it on their phone — no login — gives 1–5 stars, what they liked, and suggestions. Each link works exactly once
  4. Responses and the batch average appear on the tab for Admin, Operations and the SPOC

🔐 Permissions, Signup & Approval

Admin → Permissions / Users & Access — who can do what, decided by you

  1. Sign up: new trainers/SPOCs/staff open the Create an account link on the login page, fill their details and choose their role. They cannot log in yet
  2. Admin gets a bell alert and sees the pending signup at the top of Users & Access — Approve as-is, "Review & edit…" to correct the role/location first, or Reject
  3. Permissions tab: a grid of roles × rights (like AWS groups). Tick = everyone in that role gets the right; untick = the whole role loses it. Takes effect in seconds, no re-login
  4. The first row is the key one: who approves sheet changes — grant it to exactly the people the CEO names
  5. Special rights: open any user and expand "Special rights" to grant one person something their role does not have
  6. Admin always has every right — you cannot lock yourself out
💡 In Sheet Watch, a new row in the client's sheet shows a Create location… button: the form comes prefilled from the sheet itself, you correct anything that is wrong, and only then it enters the system. Nothing from the sheet ever writes itself into the database.

🔗 Adding Any Sheet to Watch

Sheet Watch → Manage sheets — point the system at any spreadsheet, not just the client's

  1. Open Sheet Watch and click Manage sheets. Every sheet the system is watching is listed, with when it last ran and whether that run worked
  2. Add a sheet → paste the link straight out of your browser's address bar. You do not need a special download link — the system works out the right one for Google Sheets, Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Dropbox, or any direct CSV/Excel URL
  3. Click Test this link before saving. It fetches the sheet and shows you every tab it found with its row count and column names — so you can see it is the right sheet. If it cannot open it, it tells you exactly why and what to change
  4. The sheet must be shared as "anyone with the link can view". A sheet restricted to named people cannot be opened by the server, no matter who is logged into the ERP — the server is not a person and has no Google or Microsoft account
  5. Watch mode (recommended) checks every tab on a timer and reports every changed cell. Mapped mode maps columns to ERP fields and sends changes to the Sync Inbox for approval — nothing from a sheet ever writes itself into the database
  6. Row identity columns: leave blank and the first column is used. Set them (e.g. "Institution Name, Job role") when rows get renumbered or re-sorted — that is what stops a re-sorted sheet looking like every row changed at once
  7. Click any sheet in the list to edit its link, rename it or change how often it is checked. Run now checks immediately instead of waiting for the timer
  8. Pause (untick Active) stops checking but keeps the history — for a sheet the client has stopped updating. Remove deletes it entirely; it will refuse if changes from that sheet are still waiting in the Sync Inbox, so nothing pending is lost
💡 New sheets can be added by anyone with the Configure sync sources right (Admin → Permissions). When the client sends a new or replacement sheet, nobody has to touch the code or call the developer.

🗂️ Government Attendance Import

Govt Attendance — upload the portal's attendance export and see where it disagrees with us

  1. Download the attendance report from the government portal as usual (CSV or Excel — either works, and the file does not need reformatting)
  2. Click Upload portal export and pick the file. The system reads every row, works out which centre it belongs to from the TC ID in the Org Name column, and shows you a preview before anything is saved
  3. Each row is matched to a candidate — first by the portal's own Candidate ID (CAN_…), and if that is missing, by name. Rows are labelled Matched, Ambiguous (two candidates share the name — set the Candidate ID on the right one to fix it) or Unmatched (the portal knows someone we do not)
  4. The important column is Variance: the portal's days-present minus what the centre actually logged. A positive number means the portal credits more days than our own daily logs do — that is the number the client is invoiced on, so it is the one to chase
  5. Click Import to save. Admin and Operations get an alert if anything is unmatched or shows a variance. Use the Variance and Unmatched filters on the import to jump straight to the problems
  6. Trainer rows in the file are matched to trainers, not candidates — the portal lists them alongside the trainees
  7. Tip: fill in Portal Candidate ID on the candidate record (it is on the Add/Edit form). Once it is set, every future import matches that person automatically and can never be confused with a same-name candidate
  8. Re-uploading a newer export creates a new import rather than overwriting the old one — the history of what the portal said on each date is preserved. A wrong file can be deleted

⏰ Scheme Timing & Counting Rules

Admin → Defaults — the guideline numbers, confirmed with Manish (Aug 2026)

  1. Training day: 9:00 to 18:00. A batch time slot outside this window is refused — a 7:00 AM start was asked for and is not permitted
  2. Session length: up to 4 hours per batch. A 5-hour slot is refused
  3. Sessions per day: two 4-hour batches is the sanctioned pattern. Three 3-hour batches was asked for and refused, so a third slotted session on the same day is blocked
  4. Breaks: no minimum break between sessions is prescribed, so none is enforced
  5. "Appeared" count: candidates marked Absent are counted as appeared for the client contract — they are not deducted
  6. Dropped but passed: their result is kept, but they are not billable. The Closure summary shows both the passed figure and the billable figure so the difference is never a surprise
  7. Mid-batch replacement: when someone drops out, the seat can be filled by a new candidate with a later joining date — just add them to the roster as usual
  8. Re-assessment after closing and reopening a completed batch both stay locked, as confirmed. A mistyped certificate number cannot be corrected after closure — check it before completing
  9. Uploads: the file size limit is now 100 MB (was 25 MB) so the twice-daily videos fit, and phone formats .mov and .3gp are accepted
  10. Every number above is editable in Admin → Defaults — if a scheme circular changes them, no software release is needed

❓ FAQ — Common Questions

QuestionAnswer
There's no trainer — how do I plan a batch?Create the batch without a trainer (it stays in Planning). Raise a Trainer Request from Location Detail → Trainers & Infra. The request stays highlighted on Home and under Trainers → Requests until it is Fulfilled.
A student's enrollment failed — now what?Record the reason (OTP/KYC…). The student stays in the pool — fix the issue and clear the failure, or assign them to another batch tomorrow.
Why is the attendance gap showing red?Our attendance and the government portal's differ by 10+ points. Get the biometric attendance verified at the center, and double-check the portal figure that was entered.
A new column appeared in the AVPL sheet?Add it under Admin → Sync Source → Field Mappings. Until you do, the sync stops with a "Partial" status — it never imports incorrectly.
A location has to be shut down — what happens?Via the Sync Inbox (on a sheet change) or Location Detail → Overview, set the status to "Closed" with a reason. The system generates follow-ups: stop batches, release trainers, return students to the pool — the work is only considered done once all of them are resolved.
I can't see a location/batch at allYour role is location-scoped — you only see your own centers. Ask the admin to widen your scope if needed.
A wrong entry was savedEverything can be edited within its rules (daily logs: 48 hours). Every change is recorded in the Activity tab.
Photo upload is failing (slow network)The system retries 3 times automatically, then shows a "pending uploads — Retry" button. Once the network is back, hit Retry — no need to retake the photo.
The client changed something in their sheet — how will I know?You don't have to ask them. The workbook is re-read every 30 minutes and every changed cell appears in Sheet Watch with old → new. Anything left unreviewed for 48 hours raises an alert.
The client added a brand-new centre in the sheetIt appears in Sheet Watch as an Added row with a Create location… button. The form is prefilled from the sheet; check it, correct anything wrong, then add. It never creates itself.
Someone says "I can't do X anymore"Check Admin → Permissions — that right may have been unticked for their role. Either tick it back for the whole role, or open that person in Users & Access and give them the special right individually.
A new trainer joined — how do they get access?They open the ERP link, click Create an account, choose the Trainer role and submit. You get a bell alert; approve them in Admin → Users & Access after setting their location scope. They can then log in and fill daily logs.
A candidate shows "Not eligible" — can I still enrol them?You can assign them to a batch (you'll see a warning), but you cannot complete their enrollment. Hover the badge to see why. If it's the 6-month training gap, the badge clears itself on the day they become eligible again.
The badge says "Unverified" instead of EligibleDate of birth or education is missing on that candidate. Missing information never marks someone ineligible — just fill it in and the badge resolves.
One trainer for two batches on the same day?Allowed, up to 4 batches — but give each batch a time slot (e.g. 09:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00). Overlapping times for the same trainer are blocked outright.
How do I tell a centre what to finish before a batch starts?Batches → Plan a batch → enter the start date → Copy as text or Print. It lists every task with its deadline, counted backwards from the start date.
