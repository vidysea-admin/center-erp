Center Management ERP — User Manual

One system for the whole training operation: centres, trainer hiring, candidate intake, batches, daily execution, assessment, closure and money — with the client's sheets synced in and every change audited.

Signing In & Public Pages

Staff sign in with email + password. Candidates and applicants use public pages — no account needed.

  1. Open the ERP and sign in with your email and password. New staff accounts are created by an Admin, or requested via Create an account and activated only after an Admin approves them.
  2. Candidates never need an account: /p/me shows a candidate their own training, attendance and enrollment from just their phone number.
  3. Candidate registration works two ways: a centre shares its registration link (Candidates → Portal links), or the candidate self-registers at /p/enrol — they verify their email with a 6-digit one-time code, then fill in their details and pick a centre and program. The code lasts 10 minutes; five wrong tries burn it.
  4. Trainer applicants fill the public application form at /p/trainer-apply (or the single-use link a coordinator sends from Trainers → Quick add). Their profile enters the hiring pipeline at "Fresh Lead".
Every registration or application sends a confirmation email — mails come from the ERP for real now. An Admin can mute all outbound mail from Admin → Defaults.

Roles & Access

A role is a preset rights profile. An Admin can change any role's rights (Admin → Permissions) and give or remove rights per person.

RoleWhat they hold by default
AdminEverything, always — Admin bypasses the permission matrix.
OperationsLocations, trainers & requests, candidates (create/import/assign), batches & daily logs, assessment/certification/closure, government attendance, cost entry, invoicing work, public links. Not: the sync/sheet machinery, deciding approvals, or reading the cost ledger / invoice book (they submit; the Admin reads the books).
Location (SPOC / Principal)Their own centre(s) only: locations, trainers, candidates, assignment, closure/certificates, public links. No batch editing, no attendance entry (the Trainer's job), no finance.
EnrollmentCandidates: registration and assignment, plus the enrollment worklist. No trainer directory, no hiring surface, no finance.
TrainerTheir own batches and daily logs/evidence. No candidate pool, no directory of other trainers.
  1. Three levels per right: every right in Admin → Permissions is — (none), view (read-only where the screen supports it) or edit (full). View-level is live on the finance screens; writes always need edit.
  2. Per-person deltas: open a user in Admin → Users & Access to grant special rights on top of the role, or remove a right from that one person (removal wins).
  3. View-only accounts: the "Can edit" switch off means the person can look at everything their rights allow but change nothing.
  4. Stopping people: Stop access on a user row blocks sign-in immediately. Drop… is terminal — the login dies, the email is freed for a new account, and all history stays (their activity remains readable via Activity).
Location scope is enforced by the server on every read and write (not just hidden in menus). A scoped user cannot reach another centre's records by any route — lists, single records, documents, or history.

Home (Action Center)

What needs attention today, sized to your role — KPIs, pending work, and the same numbers the detail pages will show.

  1. Cards link straight to the work: pending approvals, open trainer requests, batches not ready, today's attendance.
  2. "Today" is the Indian calendar day everywhere — early-morning entries land on the right date.

Alerts

The in-app inbox. Everything that alerts here also goes out by email to the affected people.

  1. New batch opened at your centre, signup awaiting approval, trainer application received, approval decided — each lands as an alert with a link.
  2. Alerts are scoped: a centre's people see their centre's news, not everyone's.

Locations

Every training centre: status, capacity, targets and identity.

  1. The table shows each centre with its approval and operational status. Click a row to open the centre's detail page — that is where editing lives.
  2. Institution ID is the centre's unique external identity (used by the client's sheets); it is searchable and editable on the detail page.
  3. A centre that is On Hold / Stopped / Closed stops taking new work automatically: no new batches, no new trainer nominations, no public registration.

Location Detail

One centre end to end: master fields, contacts & notes, capacity & targets, trainers & infrastructure, batches, and the full change history.

  1. Overview — edit the centre's master fields (including Institution ID). Saves are diff-based and audited.
  2. Capacity & Target — per-program targets; the reconciliation view compares the master sheet's claims with live counts.
  3. Trainers & Infra — rooms/labs and the trainers tied to this centre.
  4. Activity — who changed what, when: every field change with the old and new value and the person's name.

Trainers

The trainer directory and the hiring pipeline in one place. Open Positions shows demand; Requests track "we need a trainer here".

  1. Every trainer row shows their pipeline stage (Fresh Lead → Shortlisted → Documents Completed → Sent to NSDC → NSDC Approved → TOT Payment Done → TOT Scheduled → TOT In Progress → Certified; NSDC Rejected and Dropped are the off-ramps) and their TR ID. A Certified trainer without a TR ID shows a standing TR ID pending flag until it is recorded.
  2. Add Trainer — full profile entry. Phone must be a real 10-digit mobile (+91/0 prefixes are accepted and stored as the bare 10); email must be a real address — the form checks as you type.
  3. Quick add + send link — enter just name and phone; a single-use application link is created to send over WhatsApp/SMS (and mailed if you enter an email). The applicant fills the rest themselves.
  4. Import (Excel) — bulk upload with column mapping. Unknown columns can be accepted as extra fields; duplicates (same phone) are skipped and named; phone numbers that cannot be read as 10 digits are counted and reported, never silently dropped.
  5. Delete (Admin only) — permanently removes a junk row (test rows, duplicate imports) and its documents. Anyone referenced by a batch cannot be deleted — real people are Dropped, not erased.
  6. Click a row to open the trainer's detail page — the hiring journey lives there.
Who created a trainer shows on the row itself ("added by …") and in the Activity tab — nothing enters the system anonymously.

Trainer Detail — the Hiring Journey

CV → documents → nomination → NSDC → eligibility payment → TOT → TR ID. The stage rail shows where the person stands; every move is guarded and audited.

  1. Move to next stage — the drawer offers only the moves the rules allow from the current stage.
  2. Documents — Aadhaar, PAN, photo, CV, qualification, certificates; each uploads and verifies separately. The documents gate blocks "Documents Completed" until the mandatory set is present.
  3. Nomination — which centre × job role this person is being hired for. Required before the papers go out; a halted centre cannot be nominated for.
  4. NSDC round-trip — submission and verdict dates; a rejection records the remarks so the papers can be fixed and resent.
  5. TOT & TR ID — the eligibility payment books itself as a cost entry; certification requires the NSDC TR ID (it is what the government portal asks for when a batch is formed).
  6. Set status directly (bypass) — Admin/granted only, loudly confirmed, audited in as many words. A bypass to Certified asks for the TR ID right there (optional — the profile stays flagged until it is recorded).
  7. Activity — the full change history of this trainer with actor names.
Nothing about a missing TR ID ever blocks planning — batches can be created with a flagged trainer — but the government portal will refuse them until the TR ID is recorded. The flags exist so that surprise never happens on portal day.

Candidates

Two buckets, each with a real journey inside: Fresh (Fresh Lead → Portal Link Sent → Registered on Portal) and Enrolled (Enrolled → In Training → Assessment → Certified / Failed / Dropped — derived from recorded results, not from stale status fields).

  1. Filter by stage chips inside each bucket; search covers name, phone, alt phone, source and the portal CAN_ id.
  2. Add Candidate — phone and email are format-checked as you type (10-digit rule, like trainers). Eligibility (age / education / six-month cooldown) computes live and updates itself.
  3. Import (Excel) — map columns, preview, confirm. Possible duplicates are flagged (never blocked — one phone often serves a household); unreadable dates and un-normalizable phones are counted and reported.
  4. Portal links — generate the centre's public registration link; candidates can also self-register with an email code at /p/enrol.
  5. Documents — per-candidate uploads (ID, certificates) with per-file verify and delete.
  6. Assign to batch — multi-select candidates and place them into a batch; the batch's Enrollment tab completes the paperwork.

Batches

Plan and track batches. Trainers see their own (and can look at their centre's others); planning is for Admin/Operations.

  1. New Batch — pick centre, program and planned start. The batch code follows the standard: centre code – course – number.
  2. Trainer dropdown tells the truth: the top group is Certified with a TR ID, cleared for this centre (nomination first, capability as fallback); everyone else is listed underneath with the reason next to their name — "Certified — no TR ID yet", "Fresh Lead", "nominated: another centre". Nobody is silently hidden, and Dropped trainers are not offered at all.
  3. Skills relevant to this batch — your own recorded pick (multi-select). It never filters the trainer list; it documents what this batch is about.
  4. Time slots — a session is exactly 4 or 8 hours inside the training day (09:00–18:00). The form shows the exact rule violation while you type; the save enforces it.
  5. Earliest possible start — computed from mobilisation lead time and the trainer's availability. Pick an earlier date and the form (and the save response) warn you — nothing blocks, but you know it will not be ready.
  6. Preparation board — for every centre × program target: what is missing before a batch can start (trainer, candidates, infrastructure), with a one-click trainer request when hiring is the blocker.
  7. Plan a batch — the backward planner: give a start date, get the dated checklist of everything that must happen first.

Batch Detail

One batch end to end — Overview, Candidates, Enrollment, Daily Execution, Attendance, Closure, Feedback, Costs, Activity.

  1. Overview — dates, trainer, room, milestones (tick off the backward plan), the government batch id (SIDH) and the Drive evidence folder, reassignment while Planning.
  2. Candidates / Enrollment — roster in, paperwork done, fee state where it applies.
  3. Attendance — day-wise records; candidates can check their own at /p/attendance links.
  4. Feedback — per-student feedback links; results collect on this tab.
  5. Activity — the audit trail of the batch with actor names.

Enrollment

The worklist for getting assigned candidates fully enrolled — batch-wise, with the portal round-trip tracked.

  1. Work the queue: each row is a candidate placed in a batch whose paperwork or portal registration is incomplete.
  2. Portal state (link sent / registered / failure reason) rides on each candidate; fix and resend from here.

Daily Execution

The trainer's daily log: who was present, what was covered, photo evidence — the record the whole downstream (hours, eligibility, settlement) hangs on.

  1. Enter the day's attendance and session notes; attach evidence photos (uploads retry on flaky networks).
  2. You can edit your own log within 48 hours; Operations/Admin can edit any time. Everything is audited.

Closure, Assessment & Invoice

Batch end: assessment results, certificates, closure evidence, and the invoice work that follows.

  1. Record per-candidate results (pass/fail/absent, with reasons from the master list). The attendance threshold for assessment eligibility derives from the scheme master's hours.
  2. Upload certificates; a completed batch is nudged for its government batch id, Drive folder and slot if any are missing.
  3. Invoice work flows through the approvals queue; the invoice book itself is read by the Admin.

Government Attendance

Import the government portal's attendance export and reconcile it against the ERP's own records.

  1. Upload the export; rows match by portal ids and names, and every unmatched row is named.
  2. Differences (portal says present, ERP says absent, or vice versa) list per batch per day for follow-up.

Costs

Money entry vs the money book — deliberately separate.

  1. Entering costs (Operations and granted roles): per-centre / per-batch / per-trainer entries with categories from the master list. Trainer eligibility payments book themselves when the pipeline stage moves.
  2. Submissions go for approval; your own submissions and their state are under "My submissions".
  3. The ledger (the full book of what everyone posted) is the Admin's screen. Operations posts but does not read the book — by the CEO's design.

Sync Inbox & Sheet Watch

The client's Google/OneDrive workbooks feed the ERP. Changes queue for review; nothing applies itself to master data without an Admin's click.

  1. Sheet Watch — each watched workbook, its tabs, and what changed since last look; whole-row deletions are called out.
  2. Sync Inbox — apply or ignore each change; applied values write to the record and audit as external sync. Open a row and the Action list is built for that row: the one right step is marked ★, the ones that would be refused are greyed out with the reason. The seven actions mean:
    ActionWhat it does
    No actionDismiss it — nothing is written anywhere. The row moves to Ignored and can be re-opened later.
    Update targetOnly for a centre × job role row (approved target, TC status, TC ID). Writes that one target row. It never edits an existing batch.
    Apply valueThe plain write — copies the sheet's value into one field of the centre, trainer or candidate. If the sheet cleared the cell, applying it erases that field.
    Start locationMarks the centre operational (status becomes Active). On an approval row it also records the new approval status.
    Put on holdPauses the centre (On Hold). A reason is required. No follow-up tasks are raised.
    Stop locationStops the centre (Stopped). A reason is required, and follow-ups are raised — stop each active batch, release its trainer, cancel open trainer requests, return candidates to the pool.
    Close locationCloses the centre (Closed). A reason is required. If a batch is still Active or Closing the close is deferred: follow-ups are raised now, the centre keeps running so its batches can still be logged, and it closes when the last follow-up is settled.
    A change stays Open while it has pending follow-ups — that is what stops acknowledge-and-forget.
  3. Tab mappings (Admin → Sync Source) — map a sheet tab's columns to an entity to auto-ingest new rows. Approve mappings carefully: an active Trainer/Candidate mapping creates records from sheet rows on every sync cycle.

Admin

Programs, users & access, the permission matrix, sync sources, approvals, master lists, defaults.

  1. Programs — job-role programs per scheme; the trainer-skill field suggests from the job-roles master.
  2. Users & Access — create/approve/edit users; per-user special rights and removals; Stop access (instant), Drop… (terminal, frees the email), and Activity — what did this person do, dropped accounts included.
  3. Permissions — the role matrix, three levels per cell: — / view / edit. Applies within seconds, no re-login. Admin's own rights are fixed.
  4. Approvals — the decision queue (costs, invoices, closures, cancellations) with each request's full context; deciding needs the edit level, seeing the queue needs view.
  5. Master Lists — cost categories, drop reasons, failure reasons, job roles, and schemes (hours & amount). The per-scheme amount is visible to Admin only; hours stay open because the rules read them.
  6. Defaults — planning knobs (mobilisation lead, day window, fee rules), the outbound-email switch, and the Mail panel: the last 20 mail attempts with honest statuses — "accepted by SES — delivery not confirmed" is exactly what it says.
